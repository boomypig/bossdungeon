# Boss Dungeon

A multiplayer web-based RPG where players team up to battle bosses in a turn-based game.

## Overview

Boss Dungeon is a multiplayer game that allows multiple players to join a session hosted by a "host" player. Each player selects a character class with unique abilities and take turns to attack powerful boss enemies.

## How to Play

### Hosting a Game

1. Open the game in your browser
2. Click "BECOME THE HOST" button
3. Wait for players to join
4. Click "Start Game" when all players have joined

### Joining a Game

1. Open the game in your browser
2. Click "Become A player" button
3. Enter your name
4. Select your class (see below for details)
5. Click "Join Game"
6. Wait for the host to start the game

## Character Classes

### Samurai
- **HP**: 120
- **Damage**: 15
- **Mana**: 100
- **Abilities**:
  - Double Strike (30 mana): Strikes twice with normal damage
  - Precision Strike (40 mana): Increased critical hit chance
  - Blade Storm (60 mana): A powerful spinning attack

### Warrior
- **HP**: 100
- **Damage**: 20
- **Mana**: 80
- **Status Effect**: 30% chance to go Berserk
- **Abilities**:
  - Two Turn Attack (25 mana): Attacks twice in one turn
  - Brutal Strike (45 mana): High damage with critical chance
  - Rage Strike (50 mana): Increases berserk chance

### Astrologer
- **HP**: 80
- **Damage**: 25
- **Mana**: 150
- **Status Effect**: 40% chance to Poison
- **Abilities**:
  - Magic Burst (35 mana): High damage magical attack
  - Arcane Missiles (40 mana): Fires multiple projectiles
  - Starfall (70 mana): Massive damage with poison chance

### Prophet
- **HP**: 90
- **Damage**: 10
- **Mana**: 120
- **Healing**: 40
- **Status Effect**: 25% chance to Shield
- **Abilities**:
  - Heal (30 mana): Restores health to self or ally
  - Divine Wrath (40 mana): Holy damage to enemy

## Boss Enemies

The game features several bosses with unique abilities:

### Goblin King
- **HP**: 100
- **Damage**: 10
- **Special Ability**: Goblin Horde - Summons reinforcements when below 50% HP

### Dragon
- **HP**: 200
- **Damage**: 20
- **Special Abilities**:
  - Dragon's Breath - Massive damage to all players when below 50% HP
  - Wing Buffet - Stuns all players every 3 turns

### Dark Wizard
- **HP**: 150
- **Damage**: 15
- **Special Abilities**:
  - Dark Ritual - Heals and gains power when below 30% HP
  - Curse of Shadows - Curses all players every 2 turns

## Status Effects

- **Poison**: Deals damage over time
- **Shield**: Reduces incoming damage by 50%
- **Berserk**: Increases damage dealt by 50% but also increases damage taken by 25%
- **Stun**: Prevents action for a turn

## Gameplay Mechanics

1. Players take turns attacking the boss
2. After each player's turn, the boss attacks
3. When a boss is defeated, all players gain XP
4. When players gain enough XP, they level up, increasing their stats
5. After defeating a boss, a new boss is spawned
